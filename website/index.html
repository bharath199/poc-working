01 / What we do

Looking closely at the packages that hold modern chips

A modern device often relies on more than one chip. In advanced packaging, chips are assembled into a single package, with very fine connections between them. The smaller and denser these packages become, the more carefully each step has to be checked.

That is where our work fits. We are developing optical tools that inspect and measure packages, using light to look closely at them, so the people making packages can see what is happening at each step.

02 / How we work
